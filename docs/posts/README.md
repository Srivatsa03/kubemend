# posts

Four pieces, deliberately not the same piece four times. Cross posting identical
text gets penalized on every platform that can detect it, and Medium and dev.to
both can.

| File | Platform | Angle | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`medium.md`](medium.md) | Medium | The bug story. One narrative: the agent reverted a correct fix, and the bug was in the test setup. | ~1,350 words |
| [`devto.md`](devto.md) | dev.to | The build, start to finish. Architecture, code, the seven design steps. | ~2,300 words |
| [`linkedin.md`](linkedin.md) | LinkedIn | Short, funnels to both. Plus a second post for later in the week. | ~300 words |
| [`RECORDING.md`](RECORDING.md) | | How to record the terminal demo yourself | |

They share one bug and one tool and almost no sentences. The Medium piece is a
story with a lesson, the dev.to piece is a build log with code, and the LinkedIn
post is the hook that sends people to either.

## Order to publish

1. **dev.to first.** It indexes fast and gives you a canonical technical URL.
2. **Medium next**, same day or next. Submit to a publication (ITNEXT, Better Programming, Level Up Coding); their distribution is worth more than a personal profile's.
3. **LinkedIn last**, once both URLs exist, since the first comment links to them.

## Non negotiables in all four

- **Do not call it AI powered.** The planner is deterministic, there is no model in the decision path, and the first reply to a post implying otherwise is someone asking where the model is.
- **Never quote the 50 percent revert rate without the caveat.** The demo is built so one of its two scenarios cannot be fixed by a rollback. It shows the measurement working, not production accuracy.
- **No em dashes.**
- v0.1.0, alpha, Deployments only. Say it plainly rather than getting caught on it.

## LinkedIn reach, in order of impact

1. **Put links in the first comment, not the post body.** LinkedIn throttles posts with external links. This is the single biggest lever.
2. **Attach the GIF natively.** Video and images outperform text alone by a wide margin. If the GIF converts badly, upload the MP4 (see `RECORDING.md`).
3. **The hook has to land in two lines.** Everything after "Three of those five steps are features." is behind "see more."
4. **Answer every comment in the first hour.** That window decides distribution.
5. Two hashtags at most, or none. Tuesday to Thursday, 8 to 10am in your target market.
